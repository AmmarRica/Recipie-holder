# Chili

## Ingredients

- 2 lb lean ground beef (90/10 or 93/7)
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 Tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1½ tsp salt (to taste)
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 15 oz black beans, drained & rinsed
- 30 oz kidney beans (2 x 15 oz), drained & rinsed
- 30 oz diced tomatoes (with juice)
- 10 oz diced tomatoes w/ green chiles (with juice)
- 30 oz tomato sauce

## Steps

1. Brown ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat (4–5 min), breaking it up.
2. Add onion; cook until soft (4–5 min).
3. Add garlic, cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, oregano, salt, and pepper. Stir 30 sec.
4. Transfer to a 6-qt slow cooker.
5. Add beans, tomatoes, green chiles, and tomato sauce. Stir.
6. Cook HIGH 3–4 hrs or LOW 6–8 hrs. Taste and adjust seasoning.
